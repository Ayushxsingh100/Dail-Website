The AI and its patches

In Ask the AI, the site's one server function asks Google's Gemini (free tier) with the authors' own key, which never reaches your browser. Your request and the current configuration are sent to Google, which may use them to improve its products. Gemini's reply is an untrusted proposal: DAIL's parser, DAIL's patch validator and DAIL's orchestrator judge it in your browser, without Terraform validation. Replays from Gemini were recorded on the authors' laptop, with the date shown on each, and were also checked with terraform validate.
