Pyodide 0.27.7

Live mode runs DAIL in your browser with Pyodide, a WebAssembly build of CPython, version 0.27.7, with its sqlite3 package: pyodide.org. Self-hosted in vendor/pyodide/0.27.7/ with SHA-256 hashes (MANIFEST.sha256). Licence: MPL-2.0 (LICENSE); the Python runtime it contains is under the PSF licence.

AI-generated patches

Replays labelled “Generated by Gemini” show patches written by Google's Gemini model (gemini-3.5-flash-lite, free tier), recorded on the authors' laptop with the date shown in each replay. They are untrusted proposals: each one was checked by DAIL's parser, DAIL's patch validator and terraform validate before DAIL decided on it. The site itself never calls Gemini and holds no key.
